If you have travelled in Vietnam, you may have seen people lying back on reclined beds, eyes closed, while a therapist slowly washes and massages their head. This is **gội đầu dưỡng sinh** – the Vietnamese herbal head spa – and it has become one of the country’s favourite ways to unwind. In less than an hour, many guests say they feel as if their mind has been “reset”.

## What exactly is a herbal head spa?

The herbal head spa is a head-care ritual inspired by traditional Eastern medicine. In this tradition, the head is where many meridians and important acupressure points meet. When these areas are worked on gently and correctly, circulation improves, the body relaxes and the mind feels clear.

Unlike an ordinary hair wash, which simply cleans the hair, a herbal head spa is a **multi-step ritual**: a wash with herbal water, acupressure on the scalp, a massage of the face, neck and shoulders, and a warm wrap at the end.

## Five benefits guests love

**1. Less stress and tension headaches.** Slow, rhythmic pressure on the temples, crown and base of the skull helps the muscles of the head and neck let go.

**2. Deeper sleep.** Many guests tell us they sleep better on the night after their head spa. Warm water, the scent of herbs and a quiet room help the body switch into rest mode.

**3. A lighter neck and shoulders.** The neck and shoulder massage that comes with it is especially welcome if you spend hours at a screen – or hours on a bus.

**4. Care for scalp and hair.** Traditional herbs such as soapberry, lemongrass and ginger cleanse gently, leaving the scalp fresh and the hair naturally soft.

**5. A quiet moment for the mind.** For a while you are away from your phone and your plans – just breathing and feeling relaxed.

## The ritual, step by step

1. **Welcome with warm ginger tea** to help your body relax.
2. **A short chat** about headaches, sleep or scalp concerns so the therapist can adjust the treatment.
3. **Warm-up massage** of the neck and shoulders.
4. **Herbal wash** of the hair and scalp with warm herbal water.
5. **Acupressure on the head**: temples, crown and hairline at the nape.
6. **Face massage and a warm towel wrap** for complete relaxation.
7. **Blow-dry and rest** with a cup of herbal tea.

*Steps vary between our Relaxing Hair Wash, Herbal Head Spa and Chinese-Style Head Spa.*

| Main purpose | Clean hair | Relax the head, neck and mind |
| Products | Shampoo | Warm herbal water and gentle products |
| Massage | Little or none | Acupressure, face, neck and shoulder massage |
| How you feel afterwards | Clean | Clean, light-headed and deeply relaxed |

## Who is it for?

- Travellers recovering from long journeys and late nights
- Anyone who works with their mind and often feels stressed
- People with mild tension headaches or trouble sleeping
- Screen users with stiff necks and shoulders
- Anyone looking for a gentle, affordable treatment to try in Vietnam

**Please note:** if you have a head wound, a scalp condition, unstable blood pressure, or if you are pregnant, tell your therapist beforehand. A head spa is a relaxing wellness treatment and does not replace medical care.

## Tips for your first head spa

- **Come with an empty schedule** for an hour – rushing afterwards spoils the effect.
- **Tell the therapist your preferred pressure** – a gesture is enough if you are shy.
- **Bring a light jacket** if you visit in the evening; highland nights are cool and your hair may still feel slightly damp at the roots.
- **Leave hair styling for the next morning** – your scalp will thank you.

## How often?

To keep that relaxed feeling, once or twice a week is ideal – or whenever you feel tired and stressed. On holiday, many guests come once after arriving and again before leaving.
