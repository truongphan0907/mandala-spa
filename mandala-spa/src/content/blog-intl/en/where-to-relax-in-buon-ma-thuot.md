Buon Ma Thuot – the coffee capital of Vietnam’s Central Highlands – rewards travellers with rich coffee, powerful waterfalls and the culture of the highland ethnic villages. But after long bus rides, steep paths down to waterfalls and full days on your feet, your body needs a recharge too. Here is how to balance exploring with resting, so your highland trip is memorable rather than exhausting.

## Experiences not to miss in Buon Ma Thuot

### ☕ Coffee – the soul of the city

Start at the **World Coffee Museum** to learn how coffee is grown, roasted and enjoyed around the world, then order a strong Vietnamese coffee in one of the city’s garden cafés. Ground coffee from Buon Ma Thuot makes an excellent souvenir.

### 💧 Dray Nur waterfall

One of the most beautiful waterfalls in the Central Highlands, with white water pouring over dark basalt cliffs. There are quite a few stone steps on the way down – wear good shoes. This is usually the part of the trip your calves remember most.

### 🐘 Buon Don

Famous for its swaying suspension bridges over the river, traditional stilt houses and the culture of the highland peoples. A day in Buon Don usually means a lot of walking.

### 🛶 Lak Lake

Watch the sunset over the water, take a dugout canoe and visit the lakeside villages. It is a fairly long drive from the city, so expect a stiff back by the evening.

### 🏡 Ako Dhong village

A traditional village right inside the city, with long houses of the Ede people – lovely for a slow afternoon walk.

*Opening hours and ticket prices may change, so please check before you go.*

## Why plan an evening to unwind?

A highland trip usually combines **long drives, steep walks and plenty of steps**, plus sunny, windy days and surprisingly cool nights. The result: tired legs, a stiff back, heavy shoulders from your backpack and poor sleep in an unfamiliar bed. An evening massage, head spa or herbal soak helps to:

- Release **calves and feet** after a day of walking
- Ease the **back and shoulders** after long rides
- **Warm you up** on cool highland evenings
- Help you sleep well, ready for the next day

## A two-day itinerary with time to recharge

**Day 1 – The city**
- Morning: World Coffee Museum and a coffee break
- Lunch: local specialities
- Afternoon: Ako Dhong village
- **Evening: Vietnamese herbal head spa at Mandala** – most guests sleep like a baby afterwards

**Day 2 – Waterfalls and villages**
- Morning: Dray Nur waterfall
- Afternoon: Buon Don or Lak Lake
- **Evening: hot stone massage or a herbal foot soak and foot massage** to release your legs after a long day

If you stay longer, add an evening for a **herbal wooden-tub soak with the dry sauna** – a true taste of Asian wellness traditions.

## Tips for travellers

1. **Book a day ahead** if you are a group or would like a couple room.
2. **Choose a time after 18:00** to keep your daylight hours for sightseeing.
3. **Bring a light jacket** – highland nights are cool, especially after a hair wash.
4. **Drink plenty of water** – the highland air is fairly dry.
5. Enjoy your coffee in the morning and **skip it in the evening** if you want to sleep well.
6. Show your driver the address in Vietnamese: **86 Hàn Mặc Tử, Phường Buôn Ma Thuột, Đắk Lắk**.
